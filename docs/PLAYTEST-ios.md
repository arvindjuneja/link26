### 1.2 Haptics — on the phone, not the simulator (X7, **blocking**)

The Simulator has no haptic hardware. Every curve in `SentryCore/Feel/` is verified only by
`swift test`'s exact event times and control points; nothing has been *felt*. Wrong sharpness
or a dub 40 ms late reads as a cheap buzz.

| # | cue | how to reach it | pass = |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Heartbeat · HUNT** | play until the trace escalates to HUNT | a lub-dub at ~0.54 s, 0.75 intensity — a heart, not a buzz |
| 2 | **Heartbeat · LOCKDOWN** | escalate again | ~0.40 s at full intensity, and it **gains weight**, not only speed and sharpness (P1-8's whole point) |
| 3 | **Silence at CALM / ALERT** | a quiet board | nothing beats |
| 4 | **File a call** | hold the ring to completion | a firm settle at the end of the hold, not a click |
| 5 | **Breach thud** | miss a real threat (close a TP) | low, heavy, single |
| 6 | **Rank-up** | clear Shift 1 cleanly | the four-event pattern under the badge draw |
| 7 | **Guards** | background the app mid-HUNT; toggle Settings → Haptics off | beating stops on background; the switch silences everything |

- [ ] 1 · HUNT heartbeat
- [ ] 2 · LOCKDOWN gains weight
- [ ] 3 · silent at CALM/ALERT
- [ ] 4 · file
- [ ] 5 · breach thud
- [ ] 6 · rank-up
- [ ] 7 · guards honoured

### 1.3 The taxonomy read on the briefing

`docs/DECISION-soc-taxonomy.md` is canonical (DEF-A). The shift-intro screen is where a new
player learns it, and the failure this checks for is the one an earlier playtest already
caught once: the intro teaching one definition while the grader used another.

Read `gate/intro-medium.png`, or the briefing on the device, and confirm all three:

- [ ] **False Positive** — "the attack behaviour the rule hunts for did **not** happen; ordinary activity only looked like it"
- [ ] **Benign True Positive** — "it **did** happen, and a pentest, change ticket or known tool **sanctioned** it"
- [ ] **True Positive** — "it **did** happen and **nothing** sanctioned it"
- [ ] The debrief's grading language matches those three definitions, word for word, on at least one case of each kind

### 1.4 A full Shift 1

Seven alerts, start to finish, on the device. Roughly 12 minutes.

- [ ] Desk → **Clock in · Shift 1** → briefing → **Start the shift**
- [ ] Alert 1: pull at least two sources, read the EVIDENCE tab, file a call, read the debrief
- [ ] Coach steps 1 → 2 → 3 each advance **once** (a step must not be eaten by a sheet dismissal — P1-1)
- [ ] Work all seven alerts through to **Shift complete · 16:00 handover**
- [ ] The summary's numbers match what you actually did (accuracy, calls, missed, false escalations)
- [ ] Payout lands: standing and cash both move on the Hub
- [ ] The rank-up beat fires on the promotion, and **Continue** returns to the Hub
- [ ] Shift 1's queue row now reads `cleared · replay`, and Shift 2 is unlocked at ⬢ 40
- [ ] **Kill and relaunch mid-shift** (`docs/IOS-BUILD.md` §5): the Hub shows a Resume card and restores the exact board, `queried` set and meters
- [ ] **Reset career** (Settings → Reset career…) zeroes standing, cash and cleared boards, keeps the disclaimer acknowledged, and survives a kill + relaunch (P1-2)
